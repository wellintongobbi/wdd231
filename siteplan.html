01 — Identity

Site Name

amorae.mov

The name amorae.mov blends the Italian word amore (love) with the .mov file extension, a format associated with cameras and cinema. This combination conveys the essence of the photographer's work: capturing genuine emotions through a cinematic lens, turning fleeting moments into lasting memories. The name is short, memorable, bilingual, and unique — ideal for digital branding.

Suggested domain: amoraemov.com.br

02 — Goal

Site Purpose

The website serves as a professional digital portfolio for the photographer, with three main purposes:

1. Showcase the work. Display photographs organized by category (weddings, portrait sessions, events, and family), allowing potential clients to discover the photographer's style and quality.

2. Communicate services. Provide clear information about the types of photography sessions available, conveying professionalism and building trust with new clients.

3. Facilitate contact. Offer a contact and booking form that reduces the barrier between a client's interest and the actual hiring of the photographer's services.

03 — Target Audience

Usage Scenarios

Representative questions asked by site visitors that guide the content and features of the website:

  • What types of photography does amorae.mov offer? Does the photographer work with weddings, couple sessions, portraits, and events?
  • Can I see examples of the work before hiring? Is there a gallery organized by category?
  • How can I get in touch to request a quote or book a photography session?
  • What is the photographer's visual style? Do the photos have a defined aesthetic, such as a film-like, warm, or dark tone?

04 — Palette

Color Scheme

The palette is built around the film/analog aesthetic — warm tones of aged photographic paper as the base, with two accent colors that evoke depth and emotion.

Photographic Paper #f5f0e8 Main background across all pages
Analog Shadow #e8e0d0 Cards, secondary sections, and highlight blocks
Dark Brown #2c2416 Main text, headings, footer, and navbar
Medium Brown #6b5d4f Secondary text, captions, metadata, and paragraphs
Forest Green #4a6741 Secondary accent — badges, outline buttons, and hover links
Rosewood #6e3d41 Primary accent — CTAs, highlight borders, active wayfinding
